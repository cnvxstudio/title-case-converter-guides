# Title Case Converter: Frequently Asked Questions

These answers describe [titlecaseconverter.app](https://titlecaseconverter.app/), operated by CNVX. Product details were checked on September 23, 2026.

## Is there a free title case converter without sign-up?

Title Case Converter provides English title case and five other conversion formats without an account. Open the site, paste text, choose a format, convert, review, and copy. No subscription or AI API key is needed for the current tool.

## Does it support APA, Chicago, AP, and MLA?

Yes. The title-case options are APA 7th edition, Chicago 18th edition, AP composition titles, and MLA 9th edition. Select the standard required by your assignment or publication. These are independent implementations with editorial-review limits, not publisher endorsements.

## Does it use an LLM or send my title to AI?

No. Conversion uses deterministic browser-side rules. Your input, results, and preferred spellings are not uploaded by the tool. Website loading still involves hosting requests; [the privacy page](https://titlecaseconverter.app/privacy/) explains this distinction.

## Can it recognize every proper noun?

No. Some known names and abbreviations are preserved in title and sentence case, but unfamiliar names may need correction. Add a preferred spelling or edit the result. Do not assume capitalization proves that a name or grammatical interpretation is correct.

## Why does “with” change between styles?

For a preposition within a title, APA and AP capitalize “With,” while Chicago 18 and MLA keep “with” lowercase. Style and grammatical context both matter. See the [same-input comparison](title-case-styles.md).

## Can I paste a list of titles?

Yes, within 20,000 characters and 100 lines. Title case treats lines as separate titles. Blank lines and spaces are preserved, while line endings are normalized. This is plain-text processing, not a CSV parser or spreadsheet upload feature.

## Does it work for every language?

No. This is an English-focused formatter. For example, uppercase changes `café` to `CAFé`, not fully accented uppercase. If you need language-specific casing, use a tool designed for that language and verify its locale behavior.

## Why is Copy result unavailable after I change a setting?

The old result no longer reflects the selected format, style, or input. Select Convert to generate a current result. Copying is intended to use the reviewed result, not stale output.

## What does the replacement dialog mean?

It appears when converting again would overwrite a result you edited manually. Cancel keeps your edits; Replace applies the new conversion. Undo restores the previous state once. This protects an editorial correction such as a person's name.

## Can I share my converted text by sharing the tool link?

No. The tool link can identify a format or style, but does not include your input, result, or preferred spellings. Copy the result separately when you intentionally want to share that text.

## What if copying fails?

Browser clipboard permissions can prevent automatic copying. The tool provides a manual-copy fallback so you can select the result and copy with your keyboard. Review the copied text before using it elsewhere.

## Is it a title generator or grammar checker?

No. It preserves wording and changes capitalization. It does not invent headlines, rewrite sentences, validate grammar, or format a full reference list.

## How do I report a wrong conversion?

Email [support@titlecaseconverter.app](mailto:support@titlecaseconverter.app) with the original text, mode, style, preferred spellings if any, actual output, and expected output. If a style rule is involved, include the edition and a source. Use an anonymized example and do not send private documents.
